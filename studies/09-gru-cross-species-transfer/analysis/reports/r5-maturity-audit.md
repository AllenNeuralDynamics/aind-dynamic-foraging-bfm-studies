## Decision

All 14 admitted external cohorts were audited against their
papers and released metadata. Eleven are maturity-eligible without changing their
current canonical sample. Kwak (mouse) requires a DMSO-only adapter and complete rerun.
Miller (rat) has no defensible prespecified maturity boundary, and Alsiö (rat) measures
drug-administered acquisition/reversal rather than repeated samples of one stable
policy; both leave the mature-primary analysis.

No model job was launched during this audit. Existing Miller (rat) and Alsiö (rat)
outputs remain available only as clearly labeled sensitivity/descriptive results.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Author-defined mature, expert, or late-stage criterion
2. Released stage, phase, or protocol metadata
3. Reproducible methods criterion established before the recorded task
4. Explicit pretraining before recording when the numerical criterion is unrecoverable
5. Unresolved when no defensible maturity evidence exists

## Guardrails

- Never select a maturity cutoff using GRU or baseline performance.
- Apply maturity and treatment filters before chronological reindexing and v1/v2 splitting.
- Never adapt on one treatment and test on another.
- Preserve within-session acquisition and reversal when it is the intended mature task dynamic.
- Keep superseded all-session results only as labeled sensitivity or descriptive analyses.

## Exact adapter consequence

Kwak (mouse) currently has 39 mice, each with ten DMSO and ten CNO sessions. The
generic lexical v1 split put CNO sessions into adaptation and DMSO sessions into test,
which changes treatment at the boundary. Filtering first to DMSO retains 390 sessions
and 60,045 trials. Chronological odd DMSO sessions then provide 30,170 adaptation
trials; chronological even DMSO sessions provide 29,875 immutable held-out trials.
All existing Kwak (mouse) likelihoods and embeddings remain quarantined until that
adapter, split manifest, common-Q fit, E4 transfer, and E8 transfer are regenerated.

## Important interpretation decisions

- Zid (human) keeps all 258 main-task participants, including four constant-choice
  participants whom the paper omitted from model-based analyses. They passed the
  prespecified task-understanding screen, and removing hard-to-fit behavior would
  change the predictive estimand.
- Tang (macaque) retains within-block learning and reversal. Those dynamics are the
  mature task, not pretraining; the cohort remains descriptive because it has two
  subjects.
- Costa (macaque) is mature-eligible because lesion group is fixed between subjects,
  so adaptation and test never cross treatment. It remains a stress-test cohort.
- Eckstein (human) is post-tutorial, but the public release has 306 valid files while
  the paper reports 291 analytic participants. The existing analysis continues to
  expose that discrepancy instead of guessing an exclusion list.
- Hattori (mouse) already implements the strongest available rule: session position
  15 onward, applied before chronological reindexing and splitting.
