# r7 — Do the C3-vs-C results hold across seeds?

**Question.** r1–r6 rest on C3 seed 0. Do seeds 1 and 2 (same launch, same config) give the
same answers? Each seed's A1 table reproduces its logged likelihoods (all three, within
6e-8). Comparisons with C use C seed 0; Study 50 found C's three seeds within 0.0001
held-out likelihood of each other, and C seeds 1–2 are not extracted.

## Summary

1. **Every headline result holds in all three seeds, to the second or third decimal.**
   - C3 vs C on side choice: 0.7345–0.7351 vs 0.7338; the gain is after ignores only (gap 0:
     −0.0007 to −0.0014 nats per trial; 6+: +0.195 to +0.199).
   - Stay-after-gap prediction: within 0.007 of the animals at every gap, every seed.
   - Kernel shrinkage after a gap: lag-1 rewarded weight 0.63–0.64 after one ignore,
     0.23–0.25 after 6+.
   - The ignore rule: λ = 0.703–0.710.
   - Side relaxation under repeated ignores: 0.59–0.61 / 0.42–0.45 / 0.35–0.36 after 1 / 2 / 3;
     P(ignore) after 1 / 3 / 10 ignores identical to two decimals.
   - The ignore attractor: P(ignore) 0.973–0.983, side logit −0.25 to +0.71 (near-neutral
     against engaged attractors at ±3.2–5.3).
   - Engagement calibration (max bin gap 0.005–0.006) and P(ignore | previous ignored)
     (0.566–0.569 vs observed 0.571).
2. **Two things are seed-specific.**
   - How well the embedding decodes each mouse's ignore rate: R² 0.68 (seed 0), 0.51, 0.47 —
     always far above C's 0.01, but seed 0 is the high end.
   - The depths of the engaged-input attractors: e.g. R− +4.86 / +4.40 / +3.34 (C: +3.05).

## Corrections to earlier reports (2026-10-24)

- **r3**: its summary says the engaged attractors have "similar depths" to C's; across seeds
  they vary by up to ~1.5 log-odds (above), so no depth difference between C3 and C should
  be read from seed 0. What holds is the structure (one stable attractor per engaged input
  on its side, and the side-neutral, disengaged ignore attractor).
- **r5**, summary point 1: the embedding's R² for ignore rate is 0.47–0.68 across seeds, not
  0.68; the contrast with C (0.01) is unchanged.

## What is by construction vs what is a finding

- **By construction.** All three seeds share the data, split and config; the agreement says
  the results are not a property of one optimisation run, not that they would hold for a
  different architecture or dataset.
- **Findings.** The precision of the agreement on the ignore-specific quantities (λ, kernel
  shrinkage, relaxation, attractor) — they are properties of what the data demand, not of a
  seed.

## Caveats

- C is seed 0 only; C's seed spread is taken from Study 50's held-out likelihoods.
- The seed metrics are compact re-computations (pooled per trial rather than per mouse for
  the gap advantage; teacher-forced fit for λ), so seed 0's values differ slightly from r2/r6
  where those used per-mouse means or simulated models.
