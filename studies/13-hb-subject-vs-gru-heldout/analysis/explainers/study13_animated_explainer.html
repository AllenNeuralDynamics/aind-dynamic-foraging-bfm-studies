Study 13 in seven scenes

A two-level hierarchical Bayesian (HB) model, fitted to each held-out mouse on its own, then compared with the per-mouse MLE and the GRU. Scenes 1–6 use toy data; scene 7 shows the study's results.
Toy model in scenes 2–6: one session-level parameter θ; on each trial the mouse chooses "right" with probability σ(θ) = 1/(1+e−θ). The real study uses 5 parameters (Hattori2019) or 4 (CTT) and full foraging dynamics. Scene 7's numbers are study 13's r4 and r6 (2026-10-28). Static companion with the algebra: hb_sampling_explainer.html.
